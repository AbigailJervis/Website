How to Create a CSV File:

  1. Open Google Sheets or Excel
  2. Create two columns: "word" and "translation"
  3. Fill in your vocabulary
  4. Save as CSV (File → Download → CSV)
  5. Upload the CSV file above
  6. Click "Study" on your deck to start learning!

About This Project

I have created this section of my website because I thought the subscription to many flashcard comanies was too expensive. I didn't want another monthly subscription for something that I believed could be made in a matter of hours. So I set off to create this software to study my dutch vocab for free. Unlike the rest of my website, this section was created with the help of AI (Deepseek). This was a test for me to see what the AI agent could help me with. A current project I have is reducing subscriptions to tech bills. So, I have been trying to see if I can create software for my own usage that dosn't require a subscription or accounts and whatnot. I am excited to slowly build a catelog of programmes on my site that I (and anyone who wishes) can use for free.
